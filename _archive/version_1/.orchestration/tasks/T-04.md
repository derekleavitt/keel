# Todos: due dates and priorities

Dates, not timestamps. Due Tuesday means Tuesday in every timezone.

## Acceptance

- [x] `date` column, not `timestamp` — asserted by a test reading `information_schema`
- [x] Priority enum ordered least-to-most urgent so `desc(priority)` sorts correctly
- [x] Setting and clearing a due date are distinguishable from leaving it alone
- [x] Timezone tests pin one instant against at least three zones
