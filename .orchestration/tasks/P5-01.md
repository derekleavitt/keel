# PRD ingestion with judgment-call detection

CORRECTED FROM FIELD RESULTS. Hand decomposition measured ~40% judgment, and every
judgment call was architectural — constraints everything downstream inherits.

So this must NOT be "PRD in, backlog out". It must DETECT judgment calls, surface them
as explicit choices, and decompose only the mechanical remainder once they are settled.
Simpler to build and much harder to get catastrophically wrong.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Ingests freeform markdown
- [ ] Regression: run against docs/prd.md, it must surface the 5 known judgment calls
      (package split, unowned cross-list view, ordering strategy, scoping enforcement,
      cascade direction) and must not silently pick any of them
- [ ] Mechanical items are extracted without asking
- [ ] Ambiguities are questions, never assumptions
