# Real-time list updates

Two tabs disagreeing is a bug users report.

Proves the **realtime** row in `docs/platform-readiness.md`. A capability is not done until
the testbed genuinely uses it, a worked example exists for it, the gate covers it, and the
vendor behind it could be swapped without touching feature code.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A change in one session appears in another without a refresh
- [ ] Subscriptions are authorization-checked, not just filtered client-side
- [ ] Reconnects recover missed state
- [ ] Degrades to polling rather than breaking

## Watch for

First stateful connection. Expect the server-action model to have nothing to say
about it.

A hypothesis, not a specification. If the friction lands elsewhere, that is the more
interesting result — record it.
