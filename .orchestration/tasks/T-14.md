# Activity feed: who changed what

Required the first time a customer asks who changed something.

Proves the **audit** row in `docs/platform-readiness.md`. A capability is not done until
the testbed genuinely uses it, a worked example exists for it, the gate covers it, and the
vendor behind it could be swapped without touching feature code.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Mutations are recorded with actor, target, action and time
- [ ] Recording is not repeated by hand in every mutation
- [ ] The feed is queryable per organization and per resource
- [ ] Audit rows survive deletion of what they describe

## Watch for

Cross-cutting concern touching every mutation in every feature package. A textbook
case for something the query layer provides once, rather than each package repeating.

A hypothesis, not a specification. If the friction lands elsewhere, that is the more
interesting result — record it.
